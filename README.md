## The Problem We're Solving

- 2 in 3 American adults have at least one cardiovascular risk factor (hypertension, high cholesterol, elevated blood sugar, or high BMI)
- 1 in 3 has prediabetes — fewer than 1 in 5 knows it
- 300K+ preventable deaths per year in the U.S. are linked to poor diet
- Generic nutrition advice doesn't work for people with complex, overlapping conditions — especially in communities where cultural food identity matters

HealBites was built specifically for the gap between what generic apps offer and what this population actually needs: real personalization, real clinical context, real cultural awareness.

## What the Full Product Delivers

- **Personalized 7-day meal plan** — generated by GPT-4o from a clinical health profile (conditions, biometrics, cuisine preferences, dietary restrictions)
- **Automatic grocery list** — organized by supermarket section
- **AI nutrition chat** — context-aware assistant that knows your profile and current plan
- **Evidence-based nutrition guides** — all content reviewed by physician co-founder before publication
